About Me

Engineer turned
Experience Designer

I'm a User Experience Designer with hands-on experience builded a CRM platform for educational institutions — designing flows and interfaces used daily by admins, teachers, students, and parents in a live startup product.

My background is in Electronics & Communication Engineering, with a year as a Finance Analyst at Eaton Power Quality. That path taught me to read systems before touching a screen — how data moves, where friction hides, what actually blocks people from finishing a task.

Now I design calm, intuitive interfaces that turn complexity into something simple and human. Recent work spans EdTech CRM application, travel and fitness mobile application— moving comfortably between research, flows, and pixel-level UI.
